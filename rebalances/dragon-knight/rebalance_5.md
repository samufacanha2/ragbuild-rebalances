1. Dragon Knight

	Dragonic Pierce
	Max level : 5
	Prerequisite skills : Hack and Slasher level 7
	Group : Active
	Type : Long ranged physical
	Target: Single target
	Recovers AP : 2
	Description : Two-handed spear exclusive skill.
	Inflicts long ranged physical damage to the target for 5 hits.
	If the user is under Dragonic Aura buff, the skill will deal more damage.
	Deals additional damage depends on user's base level and POW, has a chance to trigger critical, critical chance is the user's Cri.
	The effectiveness of critical modifier is applied by half.
	_
	[Level 1] : 1450% Atk / 1600% Atk (Dragonic Aura) per hit.
	[Level 2] : 2050% Atk / 2250% Atk (Dragonic Aura) per hit.
	[Level 3] : 2650% Atk / 2900% Atk (Dragonic Aura) per hit.
	[Level 4] : 3250% Atk / 3550% Atk (Dragonic Aura) per hit.
	[Level 5] : 3850% Atk / 4200% Atk (Dragonic Aura) per hit.
