8. Windhawk

	Wild Walk
	Max level : 5
	Prerequisite skills : Nature Friendly level 3, Hawk Rush level 3
	Group : Active (AP)
	Type : Long ranged physical
	Target: Single target
	Consumes AP : 5
	Description : Used hawk to inflicts long ranged physical damage to the target for 3 hits.
	Using the gust of wind created by hawk, increase the user's evasion rate and movement speed for the certain duration.
	Deals additional damage depends on level of Steel Crow and Nature Friendly user learned, user's base level and CON, has a chance to trigger critical, critical chance is the user's Cri.
	The effectiveness of critical modifier is applied by half.
	The skill can be used while riding Warg.
	_
	[Level 1] : 4600% Atk per hit / Flee + 100, movement speed + 25%, duration : 6 seconds.
	[Level 2] : 7400% Atk per hit / Flee + 150, movement speed + 25%, duration : 9 seconds.
	[Level 3] : 10200% Atk per hit / Flee + 200, movement speed + 50%, duration : 12 seconds.
	[Level 4] : 13000% Atk per hit / Flee + 250, movement speed + 50%, duration : 15 seconds.
	[Level 5] : 15800% Atk per hit / Flee + 300, movement speed + 75%, duration : 18 seconds.
