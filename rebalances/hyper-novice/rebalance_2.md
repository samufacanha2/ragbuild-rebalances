17. Hyper Novice

	17.1. Self Study Tactics
	- Increases bonus damage of Double Bowling Bash, Mega Sonic Blow and Spiral Pierce from 10% to 15% based on level 10.
	- Increases bonus damage of Shield Chain Rush from 20% to 30% based on level 10.

	17.2. Double Bowling Bash
	- Reduces SP consumption from 72 to 65 based on level 10.
	- Increases AP recovery rate from 2 to 3.
	- Increases damage from 2100%Atk to 2650%Atk per hit based on level 10.
	- Reduces knock-back distance from 6 cells to 2 cells.
	- Increases area of effect from 7 x 7 cells to 9 x 9 cells.

	17.3. Mega Sonic Blow
	- Increases SP consumption from 45 to 60 based on level 10.
	- Increases AP recovery rate from 1 to 2.
	- Increases damage from 2300%Atk to 5350%Atk based on level 10.

	17.4. Shield Chain Rush
	- Unifies cast range to 7 cells regardless of skill level.
	- Reduces SP consumption from 65 to 62 based on level 10.
	- Increases AP recovery rate from 1 to 2.
	- Increases damage from 3400%Atk to 5100%Atk based on level 10.
	- Increases area of effect from 7 x 7 cells to 9 x 9 cells.

	17.5. Spiral Pierce Max
	- Increases SP consumption from 47 to 55 based on level 10.
	- Increases AP recovery rate from 1 to 2.
	- Increases damage from 3000%Atk to 4050%Atk based on level 10.

	17.6. Meteor Storm Buster
	- Increases SP consumption from 82 to 110 based on level 10.
	- Increases damage from 3300%/1950%(explosion)Matk to 3500%/2050%(explosion)Matk per hit based on level 10.

	17.7. Jupitel Thunderstorm
	- Increases SP consumption from 68 to 120 based on level 10.

	17.8. Jack Frost Nova
	- Increases SP consumption from 85 to 95 based on level 10.
	- Increases damage from 2200%(explosion)Matk to 5400%(explosion)Matk per hit based on level 10.

	17.9. Hell's Drive
	- Increases SP consumption from 70 to 98 based on level 10.
	- Increases AP recovery rate from 1 to 2.
	- Increases damage from 6500%Matk to 8500%Matk based on level 10.

	17.10. Ground Gravitation
	- Increases SP consumption from 88 to 100 based on level 10.
	- Increases damage from 3400%(gravitational field)Matk to 7800%(gravitational field)Matk per hit based on level 10.

	17.11. Napalm Vulcan Strike
	- Increases SP consumption from 58 to 75 based on level 10.
	- Increases AP recovery rate from 1 to 2.
	- Increases damage from 2750%Matk to 6850%Matk based on level 10.
	- Increases area of effect from 5 x 5 cells to 7 x 7 cells.

	17.12. Breaking Limit
	- Reduces bonus damage of Mega Sonic Blow and Spiral Pierce Max from 100% to 70%.

	17.13. Rule Break
	- Reduces bonus damage of Napalm Vulcan Strike from 100% to 40%.
